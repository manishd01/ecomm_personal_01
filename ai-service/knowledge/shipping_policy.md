# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Status

Orders can have the following shipping states:

- CREATED
- SHIPPED
- OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
- DELIVERED
- RETURNED

## Shipping Process

After an order is confirmed and payment is successful, the order is prepared for shipment.

The shipping workflow is:

1. Order confirmed.
2. Order packed.
3. Shipment created.
4. Tracking number generated.
5. Package handed to the carrier.
6. Package delivered.

## Delivery Estimates

Standard delivery normally takes 3–7 business days after shipment.

Delivery estimates may vary depending on:

- Destination
- Carrier availability
- Product availability
- Weather
- Holidays
- Unexpected transportation delays

## Tracking

Customers can track shipments using the tracking number associated with their order.

## Delayed Shipments

If a shipment has not been delivered within the expected delivery period, the customer can contact customer support.

The support system can check the latest shipment status before providing an answer.
