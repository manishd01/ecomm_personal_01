# Product FAQ

## Product Availability

Product availability depends on the current inventory quantity.

A product may be:

- IN_STOCK
- LOW_STOCK
- OUT_OF_STOCK

## Product Recommendations

Product recommendations should consider:

- Customer requirements
- Product category
- Price range
- Availability
- Product specifications
- Customer preferences

## Product Information

The product catalog may contain:

- Product name
- Description
- Category
- Price
- Specifications
- Available quantity
- Brand
- Warranty information

## Out-of-Stock Products

Customers cannot place a normal order for products that are currently unavailable.

If inventory is replenished, the product can become available again.

## Product Questions

Customers can ask questions about product specifications, availability, pricing, warranty, and related information.

The AI assistant should use the product catalog or approved product knowledge when answering these questions.
