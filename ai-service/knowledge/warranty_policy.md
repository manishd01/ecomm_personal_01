# Warranty Policy

## Warranty Coverage

Products covered by a manufacturer or seller warranty may be eligible for repair or replacement during the applicable warranty period.

Warranty duration depends on the product.

## Warranty Does Not Normally Cover

Warranty claims may be rejected for:

- Accidental damage
- Liquid damage
- Unauthorized modifications
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage caused by misuse
- Missing serial numbers or identifying information

## Warranty Claim Process

Customers should provide:

- Order information
- Product information
- Description of the issue
- Photos or other evidence when requested

The support team verifies the warranty eligibility before approving a claim.

## Warranty Resolution

Depending on the product and issue, the resolution may include:

- Repair
- Replacement
- Other resolution according to the applicable warranty terms
